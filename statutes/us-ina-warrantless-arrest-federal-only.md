# INA § 287(a)(2) — warrantless immigration arrest is a federal-officer power

## Excerpt

> A federal immigration officer may arrest an alien without a warrant only
> where the officer has reason to believe the alien is in the United States
> in violation of the immigration laws and is likely to escape before a
> warrant can be obtained. This warrantless-arrest authority for suspected
> civil removability is conferred on federal immigration officers only; it
> is not extended to state or local law enforcement officers.

## Formalization notes

The rule is stated from the perspective of the *state or local* officer, so
that it lines up with the Arizona provision it collides with. Federal law
grants this power to federal officers under narrow conditions and does not
extend it to state officers acting on their own — encoded here as
`prohibited` for that subject.

This is a place where the formalization compresses real doctrine: the
federal statute is written as a grant of authority to federal officers, and
the exclusivity as to state officers comes from the structure of the scheme
(as read in *Arizona*) rather than from an express prohibition.

# S.B. 1070 § 6 — warrantless arrest for a removability-triggering offense

## Excerpt

> A state or local peace officer, without a warrant, may arrest a person if
> the officer has probable cause to believe that the person has committed a
> public offense that makes the person removable from the United States.

## Formalization notes

`action: allowed` rather than `required` — the statute is permissive ("may
arrest"). That matters: under this tool's axioms `allowed` vs `required`
would *not* be a contradiction, but `allowed` vs `prohibited` is, and it is
the federal side that supplies the prohibition.

## Status

**Struck down.** Held preempted in *Arizona v. United States*, 567 U.S. 387,
408-410 (2012): it would have let state officers make immigration arrests
in circumstances where federal officers themselves could not.

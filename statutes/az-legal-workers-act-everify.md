# Legal Arizona Workers Act — mandatory E-Verify, licensing sanctions

## Excerpt

> Every employer in this state shall verify the employment eligibility of a
> newly hired employee through the federal E-Verify program. An employer who
> knowingly employs an unauthorized alien is subject to suspension or
> revocation of its business license.

## Formalization notes

`required` against the federal `allowed` — the pair is compatible, and the
tool is right to stay silent on it. Note the subject is the *employer*; the
activity is employer-side verification, which is why this never gets
compared against the worker-side employment rules.

## Status

**Upheld.** *Chamber of Commerce v. Whiting*, 563 U.S. 582 (2011): the
licensing sanctions fell within IRCA's savings clause for state licensing
laws, and the E-Verify mandate was not impliedly preempted because federal
law made participation voluntary without forbidding states to require it.

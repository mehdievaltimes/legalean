# INA §§ 287(g)(10), 642(c) — status checks with the federal government are permitted

## Excerpt

> Nothing in this subsection shall be construed to require an agreement
> before state or local officers may communicate with federal immigration
> authorities regarding the immigration status of any individual, or
> otherwise cooperate in the identification of aliens not lawfully present.
> Federal immigration authorities shall respond to an inquiry by a federal,
> state, or local agency seeking to verify the citizenship or immigration
> status of any individual within that agency's jurisdiction.

## Formalization notes

`allowed`: federal law affirmatively contemplates state officers asking the
federal government about someone's status, and obliges the federal side to
answer. This is the federal backdrop against which S.B. 1070 § 2(B) was
upheld — and the second real demonstration that `allowed` + `required` is a
compatible pair rather than a contradiction.

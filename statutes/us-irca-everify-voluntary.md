# IIRIRA § 402 — federal E-Verify participation is voluntary

## Excerpt

> Participation in the federal electronic employment eligibility
> verification program is voluntary as a matter of federal law. The
> Secretary may not require any person or other entity to participate in the
> program, except as otherwise provided for federal agencies and certain
> contractors.

## Formalization notes

`allowed`, not `required` — federal law makes E-Verify available and permits
its use, without compelling employers generally to use it. This is the
federal half of the pair that shows why `allowed` and `required` are
deliberately **not** axiomatized as mutually exclusive in
`lean/Legalean/Deontic.lean`: a state may require what federal law merely
permits without contradicting it.

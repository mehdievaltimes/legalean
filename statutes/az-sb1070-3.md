# S.B. 1070 § 3 — state crime for failing to carry registration documents

## Excerpt

> It is a class 1 misdemeanor for a person who is unlawfully present in the
> United States to willfully fail to complete or carry an alien registration
> document, if the federal registration requirements apply to that person.

## Formalization notes — a deliberate false negative

This provision and the federal one are **both** `prohibited`. They agree on
the substance, so this tool finds no contradiction and reports nothing.

The Supreme Court nonetheless struck § 3 down. The ground was **field
preemption**: Congress's registration scheme is so comprehensive that it
leaves no room for *any* state law on the subject, including one that
merely duplicates it. Arizona also allowed no probation or pardon, so its
penalty could exceed the federal one for identical conduct.

That is a different logical shape than the allowed/prohibited contradiction
this tool checks for, and it would need a new rule field (something like
`exclusive_federal_domain: true`) plus new Lean axioms to model. It is left
unmodeled on purpose rather than modeled inaccurately. See README →
Limitations.

## Status

**Struck down.** *Arizona v. United States*, 567 U.S. 387, 400-403 (2012).

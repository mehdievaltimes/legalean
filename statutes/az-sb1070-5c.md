# S.B. 1070 § 5(C) — state crime to solicit or perform work

## Excerpt

> It is a class 1 misdemeanor for an unauthorized alien to knowingly apply
> for work, solicit work in a public place, or perform work as an employee
> or independent contractor in this state.

## Formalization notes

Straightforward `prohibited`: the provision attaches criminal liability to
the worker's own act of seeking or performing work. Note the subject is the
*worker*, not the employer — that distinction is what separates this from
the E-Verify employer-side rules, which the tool correctly treats as a
different activity.

## Status

**Struck down.** Held preempted in *Arizona v. United States*, 567 U.S. 387,
403-407 (2012), precisely because Congress had decided not to impose
criminal sanctions on unauthorized workers themselves.
